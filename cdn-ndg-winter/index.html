Parking rules · per curb side

Snow loading vs posted no-parking rules ·

Every curb side of the snow-loading plan is checked at the time and weekday its snowblower reaches it, against the signs posted on that side (Ville de Montréal open data). Compliant: every pole on the side carries a no-parking or no-stopping rule valid in winter at that moment. Partly: some poles do. Not compliant: signs on the side, none bans parking at that moment (resident-permit zones, time limits, summer street cleaning…). No sign: nothing posted. Not compliant and no sign = temporary snow-loading signs needed. Operation starts at 19:00 (N1).
