How the tag figures are worked out
  • Status history comes from each task's change log in Citycare: every status change and reassignment is timestamped, so each stretch of time is counted under the status it was in and the person it was assigned to.
  • Only time inside the chosen dates counts, from the start of the first day to the end of the last, in your browser's time zone. A task still open counts up to the time of the export.
  • Hands-on is On route plus In progress. Waiting is Open, Assigned, Accepted, Paused, Blocked, Planning and Backlog. Scheduled is the time between a task's creation and its planned start, as with route patrols created ahead.
  • Hours are elapsed time between status changes, not timesheet hours. A crew that leaves a task In progress over lunch counts the lunch.
  • Tests and pilots are left out unless Include tests is on at the top of the page.
  • Tags on existing tasks were set by Baseline on 26 November 2026: patrol routes and blocks are C.SUP Rd Ptrl, potholes and AI pothole detections C.RDS Rd Plt Mtce, trip edges C.STY Crb Sdwlk Mtce, signs C.STY Sgn Mtce, debris C.CLN Litr, sweeping C.CLN Swpng. Changing a task's tag in Citycare changes it here at the next export.
